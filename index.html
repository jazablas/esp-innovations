Our role in the market

With the product from the very beginning.
All the way to the US.

ESP Innovations works alongside manufacturing companies from the earliest stage of a product. We help find the right product, develop and design it, make sure it meets US certification and compliance requirements, with third-party testing as needed, manage the logistics from the factory to the United States, and help build the marketing plan to sell it.

  1. 01

    Find the right product

    We start at the beginning: identifying products and manufacturing partners with real potential for US business buyers.

  2. 02

    Develop & design

    We work with the manufacturer to shape the product for the US, including features, materials, power, sizing and packaging.

  3. 03

    Test to exceed US standards

    We make sure every product meets US certification and compliance requirements, arranging third-party lab testing as needed, and refine samples until they meet and aim to exceed what US buyers expect.

  4. 04

    Logistics to the US

    We stay involved through production, quality checks, freight and import, moving the product from the manufacturing company to the US.

  5. 05

    Market & sell

    We help our clients put together a marketing plan to sell the product to their customers, from positioning and packaging to launch.

Questions buyers ask

Straight answers, before we start.

Don’t see your question? Ask us directly or email sales@espinnovations.com and we’ll get back to you.

Do you sell to consumers?

No. ESP Innovations works business to business only. We supply and develop products for distributors, wholesalers, retail buyers and brands, and partner with manufacturers outside the US.

Will you sign an NDA before we share our idea?

Yes. We’re happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share product details, and we keep every client relationship confidential by default.

What are your minimum order quantities?

Minimums depend on the product, the manufacturer and how much is custom. Once we understand what you need, we’ll give you the options and the minimums that come with each.

How long does it take to bring a product to market?

It depends on the stage you’re starting from. Sourcing an existing product moves faster than developing a new one with custom tooling, testing and packaging. We share a timeline with each step before work begins.

Which US certifications and testing do you handle?

We identify the requirements that apply to your product, such as FCC, UL and CPSC, and arrange independent third-party lab testing as needed. We don’t ship at scale until the product meets them. For certificates or test reports on an existing order, email compliance@espinnovations.com.

Who owns the product design and our branding?

Your brand, logo and packaging stay yours. Ownership of custom designs, molds and tooling is agreed in writing before development starts, so there are no surprises later.

Can you review our current products and help plan our next line?

Yes. In a product line review, we look at the products you sell today with your team, recommend what to keep, refresh or retire, and bring you new product ideas from our worldwide network. Then we can develop, test, certify and deliver your next line. See how it works.

How is pricing handled?

Every project is quoted for your account, based on the product, quantities, customization, testing and shipping. There are no one-size-fits-all price lists. To request a quote, email sales@espinnovations.com.

Can you help us sell the product after it’s made?

Yes. We help you build a marketing plan to sell the product to your customers, from positioning and packaging to sales materials and launch planning.

PC makers move some production back to China as AI drives up part costs

HP, Asus and Acer are shifting some laptop and desktop production from Thailand and Vietnam back to China. AI data centers are soaking up memory and other components, which is driving up prices.

Why it matters to buyersWhen component costs rise, where and with whom you manufacture makes a big difference. Factory relationships and fast quotes matter more than ever.

ComplianceOct 28, 2026

New York requires security features on internet-connected baby monitors

A new law bans the sale of new internet-connected baby monitors in New York unless they include features that block strangers from watching or listening. Packaging must also carry a clear warning label.

Why it matters to buyersConnected products face more state rules. Security features and labeling need to be built in from the design stage.

Google unveils Gemini 4 Carbon, its newest frontier AI model

Google says Carbon makes big gains in coding, long-running tasks and cybersecurity. It's rolling out first to a small group of cybersecurity teams before wider release.

Why it matters to buyersAI models are improving month by month, and the products built on them, from assistants to smart devices, are improving just as fast.

Samsung used AI and 119 million ear data points to design its first open-ear earbuds

The $229 Galaxy Buds On clip onto the ear instead of sitting inside it. Samsung refined the fit with AI-assisted optimization and physics simulations, and the earbuds work with Gemini Live hands-free.

Why it matters to buyersAI is moving into product development itself, cutting the time it takes to test fit, comfort and form.
